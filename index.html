    What Keisha mode grades against
    • Head count 8–8.5 heads. Crotch 47% of her height from the floor.
    • Hips ÷ shoulders about 1.5. Waist ÷ hips about 0.62.
    • Side view: breasts 0.1–0.5 heads past her belly; belly hangs 0.3–0.8 heads below the navel; butt 0.6+ heads past her upper back; lowest point of the breasts within 0.3 heads of the navel.
    • Extra checks: navel 3 heads down; knee 2 heads up; wrist at crotch level; hand about face length; neck 0.75–1.0 of head width; head 0.28–0.38 of shoulder width; foot about 1.2 heads.
    • Height is shown as a note only. It is never graded.
    How to get a fair reading
    • Straight-on, full-body shots measure best. A low camera makes legs look longer; a high camera makes them shorter. A close, wide shot makes whatever is nearest the camera look bigger.
    • She stands straight with feet flat. No bent knees, no leaning. Set Footwear if she has shoes on.
    • Crown means the top of her skull. Ignore the bun and hair volume. Chin means the bottom of the chin bone.
    • Turned at an angle? Heights still work up to about 30°. Uncheck Widths past about 15°, because some of her side shows and widths read too wide.
    • Can't see a point (cropped, hidden by a counter, in the sand)? Press "Skip this point". Results that need it just won't show.
    • Real height needs something of known size standing on the same floor. Without one, the picture alone can't show how tall she is.
    • Two references are better than one: if they disagree, the AI drew one of them at the wrong size.
    • Eye level is the height of the camera, drawn as a flat line across the picture. On a beach it's where the water meets the sky. Indoors, edges that run away from you (floor tiles, ceiling, counter edges) slant toward each other; the height where they'd meet is eye level. "Find eye level for me" does that math from two edges.
    • Lying down: measure only when the camera looks straight down at her or straight from the side. A mattress length makes a good ruler.
    • Sizes from a front picture alone are shown as a range. Add a side picture of the same character for single numbers, cup size and shoe size.
    • Clothes add width. Measure sizes on fitted clothing or none.
    • "Save marked-up picture" saves to Files (Downloads) on iPhone, not to Photos.
