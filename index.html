    What Keisha mode grades against
    • Head count 8–8.5 heads. Crotch 47% of her height from the floor.
    • Hips ÷ shoulders about 1.5. Waist ÷ hips about 0.62.
    • Side view: breasts 0.1–0.5 heads past her belly; belly hangs 0.3–0.8 heads below the navel; butt 0.6+ heads past her upper back; lowest point of the breasts within 0.3 heads of the navel.
    • Extra checks: navel 3 heads down; knee 2 heads up; wrist at crotch level; hand about face length; neck 0.75–1.0 of head width; head 0.28–0.38 of shoulder width; foot about 1.2 heads.
    • With no reference object, Keisha mode uses her spec head size (6′11″ ÷ 8.25 heads) as the ruler.
    • Height is shown as a note only. It is never graded.
    How to get a fair reading
    • Press on the picture and slide: the magnifier shows exactly where the point will land. Lift to drop it. The last point you placed has a gold ring: fine-tune it with the nudge arrows (arrow keys on a Mac; Shift for bigger steps). To redo any point, tap it in the list. Once every point is placed, you can also drag points.
    • Pinch, or use the zoom slider, to zoom up to 16×. Two-finger drag (or trackpad scroll) moves around. Fit resets.
    • Tap any item in the list to redo it, even one you skipped.
    • Straight-on, full-body shots measure best. A close, wide shot makes whatever is nearest the camera look bigger.
    • Crown means the top of her skull, not the bun. Chin means the bottom of the chin bone.
    • Turned at an angle? Turn on Turn angle and click her face edges and nose tip. Widths get corrected. Past about 45°, widths can't be trusted.
    • Real height needs something of known size. Two references are better than one: if they disagree, the AI drew one at the wrong size. Small things (phone, can, card) are for close-ups.
    • Eye level is the height of the camera. On a beach it's where the water meets the sky. Indoors, "Find eye level for me" works it out from two edges that run away from you.
    • Beach or open field with nothing to measure against? Set Reference 1 to "Nothing to measure against", turn on the horizon option, pick how high the camera was, and click the horizon. It gives an Estimate: good to a few inches when the camera was level and the sand is flat.
    • Lying down: measure only when the camera looks straight down at her or straight from the side.
    • Sizes from a front picture alone are ranges. Add a side picture of the same character for single numbers, cup size, volumes and shoe size.
    • Clothes add width. Measure sizes on fitted clothing or none.
    • "Save marked-up picture" saves to Files (Downloads) on iPhone, not to Photos.
