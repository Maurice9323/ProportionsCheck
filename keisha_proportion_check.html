    How to get a fair reading
    • Only judge straight-on, full-body shots. A low camera makes legs look longer; a high camera makes them shorter.
    • She stands straight with feet flat. No heels, no bent knees, no leaning.
    • Crown means the top of her skull. Ignore the puff bun and hair volume; guess where the skull curve is.
    • Chin means the bottom of the chin bone, not the soft fold under it.
    • Widths use only left-to-right distance. Click the body outline, not her arms or hands.
    • Heights use only up-and-down distance, so a slightly off-center click won't change them.

    Green, yellow and red are tolerance bands set around the bible's numbers. Green is on target, yellow is close enough to fix with a pass, red means pick a different picture.
